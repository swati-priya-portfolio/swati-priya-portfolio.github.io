What I’d Defend. What I’d Change.

Strong product work means defending the choices that held — and naming what still needs evidence.

What I’d defend

01

Calculator-first onboarding

Earn attention with a useful answer before asking for commitment.

02

Radical transparency

Exclusions and uncertainty belong in the decision, not the fine print.

03

Designing for the household

The product is used by a parent, but the decision belongs to a family.

Test

What I’d change

01

Validate earlier

Test the trust sequence before polishing the entire journey.

02

Instrument from day one

Capture question drop-off, report sharing and return behaviour at launch.

03

Validate comprehension + consent

Measure whether families understand the recommendation, not only whether they continue.

Build trust → explain the model → make the next step feel earned.

Strong product work is clear about what to test next.
