The problem

Too much to remember

Plan pages used different labels, jargon and long benefit lists.

Why compare

Put differences in one place

Parents inspect plans without carrying details across tabs.

Design decision

Select → choose → add → review

Up to three plans. Editable selections. No table until the set is ready.

Principle + measure

Recognition over recall

Progressive disclosure lowers early load. Track compare-to-shortlist progression.

Progressive disclosure kept the comparison manageable: shortlist first, details second.
