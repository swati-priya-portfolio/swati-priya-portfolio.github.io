06 · Feature audit

Four Tools. Four Trust Breaks.

The audit clarified what to remove before adding anything new.

01

Health Insurance Calculator

Estimate the right cover for a family.

Problem
Asked for effort before earning trust.
Pattern
Parents want an answer first, not a questionnaire.
Response Show the answer before asking for anything.
02

Compare Health Plans

Compare relevant policies side by side.

Problem
Too many plans create paralysis.
Pattern
More choice reduced confidence.
Response Fewer options. Clearer differences. Faster trust.
03

Family Suraksha Report

Translate inputs into a personal risk story.

Problem
Generic reports feel irrelevant.
Pattern
People trust a picture of their own risk.
Response Make the risk personal, or it is just noise.
04

Eligibility & Rejection Guidance

Make exclusions visible before purchase.

Problem
Hidden exclusions break trust.
Pattern
Trust grows when less is hidden.
Response Say what you do not cover, first.
