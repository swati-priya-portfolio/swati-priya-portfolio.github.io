Four Tools.Four Trust Breaks.

01

Health Insurance Calculator

Helps parents estimate the right cover for their family.

Problem

Asked for effort before earning trust

Pattern

Parents want an answer first, not a questionnaire.

Response

Show the answer before you ask for anything.

02

Compare Health Plans

Helps parents evaluate and choose the best plan.

Problem

Too many plans create paralysis

Pattern

Too many choices reduce confidence, not increase it.

Response

Fewer options, clearer differences, faster trust.

03

Family Suraksha Report

Shows the financial impact of an unexpected event.

Problem

Generic reports feel irrelevant

Pattern

People trust a picture of their own risk over expert advice.

Response

Make the risk personal, or it’s just noise.

04

Eligibility & Rejection Guidance

Understanding rejection builds transparency & trust.

Problem

Hidden exclusions break trust

Pattern

Trust isn’t built by promising more — it’s built by hiding less.

Response

Say what you don’t cover, first.

The audit clarified what to remove before adding.
