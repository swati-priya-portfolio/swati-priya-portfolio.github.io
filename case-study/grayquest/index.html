01 / PROBLEM & APPROACH

SCHOOL FEES WERE THE GOAL. THE APPLICATION WAS THE CHALLENGE.

Parents needed to compare a financial commitment, understand verification, and know what remained after payment. I explored these questions through parent and institute calls, then iterated with product managers and the CEO.

01 / CHOOSING A PLAN

What do I pay today and every month?

Payable today, monthly instalments, duration and applicable fees needed to be easy to compare.

02 / VERIFICATION

Why are these details needed now?

Sharing financial details needed an explanation of the check and a route forward if it failed.

03 / REPAYMENT SETUP

It says pending. What should I do?

Payment success, repayment setup and application review needed to communicate different states.

REQUIREMENTS → EARLY FLOWS

I gathered requirements with product managers and spoke with parents and institutes. I explored ideas through flowcharts and low-fidelity wireframes, then reviewed the sequence with the PM and CEO.

FEEDBACK → FINAL UI

Stakeholder feedback guided further research. I studied banking journeys to understand similar checks, refined the flow and screens with the PM and CEO, and handed the designs to developers.

Who is applying?

Can someone else in our family apply?

PARENTS & GUARDIANS

For education up to Class 12, parents typically managed the application and repayments for their child.

STUDENTS APPLYING FOR THEMSELVES

Undergraduate and postgraduate students also used the platform, more proactively choosing “I am the Student” and completing their own education-loan journey.

ANOTHER APPLICANT, ANOTHER ASSESSMENT

An unsuccessful application need not end the journey. Another parent, guardian or the student can apply with their own details and applicable checks; approval is assessed separately.

14 / DESIGN RESPONSE

WHAT THE DESIGN RESOLVES.

Three decisions balance comparison, commitment and progress. Each leaves a specific question to validate.

COMPARE

“Which plan works for me?”

MAKE PLANS COMPARABLE

A lower EMI can mean a longer commitment. Amount and duration support comparison; total cost and fees also need to be clear before commitment.

PAYMENT

“What do I pay today?”

SHOW THE IMMEDIATE COST

The amount due today belongs before payment-method selection. Parents still need to understand how that payment relates to future instalments.

STATUS

“Am I actually done?”

SEPARATE ACTION FROM REVIEW

Payment, repayment setup and approval are distinct milestones. A pending state needs to explain whose action comes next.
